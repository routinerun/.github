## Routine Run

An iPhone and Apple Watch app that walks you through a routine one step at a time.
Describe what you do in a sentence; it writes the routine, then puts the next step on your
Lock Screen and your wrist. No account. **[routinerun.com](https://routinerun.com)**

- **[routine-run](https://github.com/routinerun/routine-run)** — the method, help, the API,
  MCP, the Claude skill, and catalogues of routines, connections and shortcuts. Issues welcome.
- **MCP:** `https://api.routinerun.com/mcp` (your routines, after a code on the phone) and
  `https://api.routinerun.com/mcp/public` (write a routine, no sign-in).

The app's own code is not here, and that is deliberate.
